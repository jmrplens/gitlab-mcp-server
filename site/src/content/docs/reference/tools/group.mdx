Everything done to a group: create, read, list, search, update, archive, transfer, delete and restore it; its subgroups, projects and issues; its members and its sharing with other groups; its labels, milestones, boards and badges; uploads and its avatar; export and import; its releases; and its service accounts with their personal access tokens. Labels, milestones, boards, members and the rest have no group of their own: each is a set of actions here.

From Premium the group also carries webhooks, push rules, protected branches and environments, epics with their notes, discussions, issues and boards, group wikis, LDAP and SAML group links, SSH certificates, billable members and activity counts; from Ultimate, the credentials inventory and security settings.

`group.transfer` needs the Owner role on both the group being moved and its new parent; `group.transfer_locations` lists the parents it can go under. Since GitLab 19.4 GitLab applies the transfer in the background (GitLab 18.11 to 19.3 do so when the `groups_and_projects_async_transfer` feature flag is enabled), answering with the group where it still is, and the action reads the group back every 2 seconds for up to 45 seconds. A failed move is reported only as a pending to-do item of action `transfer_failed` for the user who asked for it, which the action turns into an error; a move that has neither landed nor failed by then is answered with `transfer_queued` set. Read the group back later with `group.get`, look for that item with `user.todo_list`, and do not send the transfer again, since GitLab runs a second transfer as well while the first is queued.

## Sample questions

- "List my GitLab groups"
- "Who are the members of group platform?"
- "Share group platform with the developers group"
- "List the open epics of group platform"

## How to call it

- **Dynamic**, the default surface: call `gitlab_execute_action` with `action` set to the action's ID, such as `group.analytics_issues_count`, and its parameters in `params`. `gitlab_find_action` finds an ID from a description of the task.
- **Meta** (`GITLAB_MCP_TOOL_SURFACE=meta`): call `gitlab_group` with `action` set to the action's name, such as `analytics_issues_count`, and its parameters in `params`.
- **Individual** (`GITLAB_MCP_TOOL_SURFACE=individual`): call the action's own tool, such as `gitlab_get_recently_created_issues_count`, with its parameters as the arguments.

### `group.analytics_issues_count`

> Get the count of recently created issues across a group. Returns: the group path and recently created issues count (Premium). See also: [`group.analytics_mr_count`](#groupanalytics_mr_count), [`group.analytics_members_count`](#groupanalytics_members_count), [`group.get`](#groupget).

- **Meta-tool**: `gitlab_group`, action `analytics_issues_count`
- **Individual tool**: `gitlab_get_recently_created_issues_count`
- **Tier**: Premium
- **Classic or OAuth token**: `read_api` (or `api`)
- **Fine-grained token**: Activity Analytics: Read at group ([permissions page](/gitlab-mcp-server/reference/fine-grained-permissions/#group))
- **Behavior**: read-only, idempotent

| Parameter    | Type     | Mandatory | Description                                            |
| ------------ | -------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| `group_path` | `string` | yes       | Full path of the group (e.g. my-group or parent/child) |
