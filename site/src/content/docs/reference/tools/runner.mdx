Runners: list the ones the user owns, every runner of the instance (an administrator's), a group's or a project's; read, update (pausing included) and remove one; list its jobs and its managers; enable or disable it for a project; register one with a registration token, verify a runner authentication token, and delete a runner by its token; and reset the registration tokens of the instance, a group or a project, or a runner's own authentication token.

Runner controllers, the experimental control plane administrators use to manage runners, are the actions served from Ultimate: controllers, their scopes and their tokens.

## Sample questions

- "List the runners available to project 42"
- "Show the details of runner 5"
- "Which jobs has runner 5 run today?"
- "Pause the runner tagged gpu"

## How to call it

- **Dynamic**, the default surface: call `gitlab_execute_action` with `action` set to the action's ID, such as `runner.controller_create`, and its parameters in `params`. `gitlab_find_action` finds an ID from a description of the task.
- **Meta** (`GITLAB_MCP_TOOL_SURFACE=meta`): call `gitlab_runner` with `action` set to the action's name, such as `controller_create`, and its parameters in `params`.
- **Individual** (`GITLAB_MCP_TOOL_SURFACE=individual`): call the action's own tool, such as `gitlab_runner_controller_create`, with its parameters as the arguments.

## Availability

How many of these actions an instance serves at each tier, out of a total of 34:

- **Free**: 19
- **Premium**: 19
- **Ultimate**: 34

Read-only actions: 13 of 34, the ones a deployment in read-only mode keeps. A token with `read_api` and not `api` is served 15 of the 34.
