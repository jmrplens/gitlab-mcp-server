What a review does to a merge request: read its changes, its diff versions and its raw diffs; read and write its notes; open, reply to and resolve threaded discussions, inline on a line of the diff or general; and queue draft notes that stay private until they are published, one at a time or all together as a single review.

Approving, merging and the rest of the merge request's lifecycle are on [Merge requests](/gitlab-mcp-server/reference/tools/merge-request/), and so are award emoji on its notes.

## Sample questions

- "What changed in merge request !15?"
- "Comment on line 42 of src/app.go in merge request !15"
- "Resolve the open threads I started on merge request !15"
- "Publish my draft review"

## How to call it

- **Dynamic**, the default surface: call `gitlab_execute_action` with `action` set to the action's ID, such as `mr_review.changes_get`, and its parameters in `params`. `gitlab_find_action` finds an ID from a description of the task.
- **Meta** (`GITLAB_MCP_TOOL_SURFACE=meta`): call `gitlab_mr_review` with `action` set to the action's name, such as `changes_get`, and its parameters in `params`.
- **Individual** (`GITLAB_MCP_TOOL_SURFACE=individual`): call the action's own tool, such as `gitlab_mr_changes_get`, with its parameters as the arguments.

## Availability

Every tier serves the whole group, on self-managed instances and on GitLab.com alike.

Read-only actions: 10 of 23, the ones a deployment in read-only mode keeps. A token with `read_api` and not `api` is served 10 of the 23.
